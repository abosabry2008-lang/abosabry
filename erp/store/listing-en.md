## Full description
Abosabry ERP is a practical enterprise resource planning app for managing a trading business from one place.

Features:
• Products, inventory, warehouses and per-item stock movements.
• Customer orders, invoices and returns, with A5 invoice printing.
• Purchase orders, suppliers and purchasing.
• Customers, account balances and statements.
• Treasury, payments, collections and transfers between cash boxes.
• Customer/supplier settlements and expense categories.
• Sales, purchasing, profit, inventory, tax, trial balance and balance-sheet reports.
• Role-based user permissions with full Arabic and RTL support.
• Fast responsive interface for phones and tablets.

The app is intended for business use and requires an account created by the organisation's administrator. There are no ads, and no real payments or card processing are handled inside the app.
